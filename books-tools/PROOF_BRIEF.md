## What to check and fix, per page
1. **Completeness:** every sentence, footnote, table row, equation, list item of the printed page is present and in order (Lévy: translated faithfully,
   nothing added, nothing left out; technical terms consistent with the rest of the book; Wolfe: verbatim, original spelling kept).
2. **Equations:** compare every symbol (letters, case, primes, sub/superscripts, signs, fractions, exponents, equation numbers) with the zoomed scan.
3. **Numbers and tables:** every value and unit; tables cell by cell.
4. **Figures:** every figure referenced exists, is placed after the paragraph that cites it, its caption matches the printed number; the crop shows the
   whole figure (all labels) and nothing else. Re-crop when clearly bad:
   `/usr/bin/python3 -I <Books>/tools/pagetool.py crop <key> <page> x0 y0 x1 y1 WORK/out/<id> <name> [--white …] [--rot 90]` then Read the preview.
   Lévy II plate figures (Pl. XXVII–XXXII) are NOT in the scan — keep their placeholder notes.
5. **Page markers:** each `<sub>page N</sub>` sits where page N starts.
6. **Footnotes and corrections:** the author's misprints stay as printed with a footnote saying what the scan shows; a correction the transcriber made
   silently to the author's text must be restored (or footnoted). Uncertain → best reading + `[uncertain reading]` footnote.

## House rules
- All text is English (original titles may stay beside the English).
- Never write anything about AI, models, Claude, Opus or agents in any file (logs included).
- Every figure showing a structural system must carry `[labels: …]` in its alt text (cables, arches, vaults and shells, truss, beam, frames, plates or slabs, columns, bracing, material-specific forms, plastic and stress field models, connections, graphic statics) — check the labels match the drawing and fix them.
- Keep ALL temp files in WORK/tools-<yourjob>/ and never delete outside it.

## Build and verify
`/usr/bin/python3 -I WORK/out/<id>/src/build.py WORK/out/<id>/src/<file>.md` → `unconverted: 0`, `unknown inline commands: none`.
Read every equation sheet it prints (`WORK/out/<id>/.eqpng/sheet-<stem>-NN.png`) and compare each rendered equation with the scan.
Unsupported in build.py: `\phantom`, `\textit`, `\displaystyle`, `\text` inside `\tag`, `cases`, `array`, `matrix`, `\operatorname`; inline `\mathfrak` (use the Unicode 𝔐).

## Log and report
Write `WORK/out/<id>/REVIEW-proof-<stem>.md`: pages proofread (list), counts of fixes by kind (text, equation, number/table, figure, marker,
footnote), a table of every fix (page, before → after, reason), remaining uncertainties, and anything you could NOT check. Be honest.
Report to the lead in ≤ 15 lines.

## Lessons (October 2026)
- When several proofreaders share one file, each edits only its page range with small targeted edits and re-reads before every edit;
  never rewrite the whole file with a script.
- Recompute numerical tables from their column formulas; footnote printed values that disagree ("As printed …; the formula gives …").
- Restore silently "corrected" author text to what the scan prints and footnote the intended reading.
- Report terminology that differs between translators instead of changing other proofreaders' ranges; the lead does one consistency pass.
